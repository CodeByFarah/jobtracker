JobTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

JobTrack is a personal job-application tracker. This page explains what data the app handles, why, and how you can remove it.

Data you enter

When you create an account we store your name, email address and a hashed password. The applications, companies, interviews and notes you add are stored in our database so you can see them when you sign in. They are visible only to your account.

Gmail access (optional)

If you choose Connect Gmail, JobTrack asks Google for read-only access to your mailbox (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). JobTrack cannot send, delete or change your email.

  • What is read: recent emails (up to the last 180 days on the first check, then new ones periodically) are scanned to find messages about your job applications, such as application confirmations, assessments, interview invitations, offers and rejections.
  • How it is used: the sender, subject and the beginning of the email body are analysed by an AI model run by JobTrack itself to decide which application an email belongs to and what it means, so your applications' statuses can be updated automatically.
  • What is stored: for job-related emails only, we keep the Gmail message ID, sender, subject, date, a one-sentence summary and the detected status. We do not store full email bodies. Emails unrelated to your applications are not stored.
  • Access token: the Google access token is stored encrypted and is used only to read your mailbox for the purpose above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JobTrack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the job-tracking features you see in the app. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not used to train AI models.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. It is processed only by the services that host the app: the website (GitHub Pages), the server (Render) and the database (Neon).

Removing your data

  • Disconnect Gmail in the app at any time: this revokes JobTrack's access at Google and deletes the stored token. You can also remove access from your Google Account permissions.
  • Delete your account in the app's settings to remove your account and everything stored with it.
